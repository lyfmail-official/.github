# LYF Mail — Privacy-First Digital Wellness

**Every Choice Shapes Tomorrow**

LYF Mail is a privacy-first digital wellness platform based in **Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India**. Since 2020, we have delivered free curated newsletters and privacy-first tools to 50,000+ subscribers across India — helping people make better decisions about their **health, wealth, career, safety, and mental wellbeing**.
